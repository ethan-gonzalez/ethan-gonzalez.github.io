Research

PFAS Research

Michigan State University · High School Honors Science, Math & Engineering Program · Jun – Dec 2023

During my research at Michigan State University’s Center for PFAS Research, I conducted a molecular dynamics (MD) study titled “Impact of Electric Fields on Aqueous Phase Contaminants,” investigating how varying electric field strengths influence the diffusion and coordination of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) such as PFOA and PFBA in water. Using LAMMPS for MD simulations with CLAYFF, AMBER, and SPC force fields, I constructed periodic simulation boxes containing over 12,000 water molecules and analyzed the systems using VMD, Python, and Matplotlib to compute radial distribution functions and mean square displacement–based diffusion coefficients.

My results showed that increasing electric field strength decreases PFAS diffusion and revealed the formation of a PFOA dimer under low-field conditions, providing new insight for PFAS remediation strategies. I presented my findings to a board of professors and postdoctoral researchers and was honored with the Scholar Award of the Regeneron Science Talent Search for this work.

Project Wavefront

Wavefront CAD

Project Wavefront · Jul 2025 – Present

Led CAD (Creo) design of the 1st undergraduate-designed additively manufactured rotating detonation rocket engine system.

At Project Wavefront, I led the CAD design of the Mark 1 GRCop-42 rotating detonation rocket engine using PTC Creo, optimizing the system for L-PBF manufacturing constraints while meeting performance targets defined by our team’s research and final design specifications. I iterated on the full engine assembly during biweekly design reviews, incorporating insights from FEA, CFD, and thermal analyses conducted by teammates to improve structural integrity, manufacturability, and combustion performance.

Using both simulation results and hand calculations, I refined coolant channel geometries to improve flow distribution and thermal efficiency, optimized the injector and combustion channel design to maximize specific impulse, and developed a novel, repeatable ignition system to ensure reliable detonation initiation. Throughout this process, I applied FOS and DFM principles to maintain structural and manufacturing feasibility.

As a founding member of Wavefront, I have interviewed and onboarded new members, shared guidance on top-down modeling techniques and simulation workflows, and maintained active communication with L-PBF manufacturers to discuss print feasibility, material availability, and production timelines. Working alongside such driven and creative teammates, I have learned to blend detailed mechanical design with collaborative problem solving to bring our additively manufactured rotating detonation rocket engine closer to reality.

Project Wavefront

Wavefront CFD

Project Wavefront · Ansys Fluent

In addition to leading the overall engine design effort, I conducted CFD analyses in Ansys Fluent to study flow distribution, absolute pressure, velocity, and thermal performance within the regenerative cooling channels. I validated areas of maldistribution in both pressure and velocity, then incorporated these results into successive design iterations to improve cooling uniformity and minimize localized overheating.

This process involved generating high-quality meshes, simplifying geometries to balance simulation accuracy with computational efficiency, and applying the results directly to modify channel cross sections and flow paths. Through this iterative meshing, analysis, and design revision workflow, I developed a stronger intuition for model optimization and established a reliable feedback loop between CFD data and mechanical design decisions.
